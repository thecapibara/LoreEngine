Aliases: Ser Aldric, The Dark Knight
Tags: npc, mercenary

Ser Aldric Vane - the "Dark Knight", a wandering mercenary with a cursed blade.

Aldric arrived in the valley three winters ago and never spoke of his past.
He takes contracts no one else will touch, and always pays his tab at the
Old Tavern in full. The locals say his sword whispers at night.
